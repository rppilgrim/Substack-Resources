# IRB Data Request Log

## Overview

A single log of every data request that comes through the institutional review board, with what was asked for, what was released, and under which approval. Replaces an email thread and a shared inbox.

## Driving Questions

- What is the current turnaround time from request to data release?
- Which requests are waiting on us and which are waiting on the requester?
- What data elements get requested most often, and should they be a standing extract?

## Notes

- The pause rule matters a lot. Raw elapsed time made us look far slower than we are, because most long requests are long while sitting with the requester.
- Roughly a third of requests ask for the same handful of elements. Those are the candidates for a standing extract.
