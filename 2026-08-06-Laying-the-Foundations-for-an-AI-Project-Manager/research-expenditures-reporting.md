# Research Expenditures Reporting

## Overview

Annual research expenditure reporting, assembled from the finance system and reconciled against sponsored programs records before it goes to federal survey submission. Accuracy matters more than speed here.

## Driving Questions

- What did the institution spend on research this year, by field and by funding source?
- Where do finance and sponsored programs disagree, and why?
- How does the expenditure trend compare to peer institutions?
- Which fund groups moved between years, and does the prior year need restating?
- What share of expenditure passes through to subaward recipients?

## Notes

- The subaward variance is a timing difference, not an error: finance books on invoice receipt and sponsored programs books on the award period.
- Peer comparison is the part leadership always asks about first, so it is worth having ready before the sign off meeting rather than after.
- Cost share is reported on its own line of the survey, so it has to stay out of the main reconciliation or the gap reads as roughly twice its real size.
- The peer set is frozen for this cycle. Requests to swap institutions mid cycle go to the next one, otherwise the trend stops being comparable to what was filed.
