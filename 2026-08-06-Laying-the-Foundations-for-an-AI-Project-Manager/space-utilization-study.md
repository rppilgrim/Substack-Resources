# Space Utilization Study

## Overview

How classroom and lab space is actually used across the week, to support the capital planning process and the current classroom renovation program.

## Driving Questions

- What proportion of classroom hours are in use, by building and by time of day?
- Which rooms are consistently booked but lightly occupied?
- Does the current room inventory match the section sizes being scheduled?
